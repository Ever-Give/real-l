Female rock climber on a long multi-pitch route

Author: LunaRae

*000*2020-07-22 05:18

Here's a story from a few years ago when me and three friends (two of them a couple) went climbing together. As a group we woke up super early at my friend's place and had some cereal/coffee/quick breakfast then drove to an area with a lot of nice lines near their house. We found the start of the route we were planning easily enough since they'd both climbed it before: a 5.11b, with 8 pitches of mostly slab with a bit of "easy" crack climbing. Our early start ended up being a bit pointless since we opted to wait anyways until noon-ish to begin climbing to give the rock time to dry.

We'd all gone into the woods a bit for a pee shortly before starting the first pitch for the convenience of it since peeing while wearing a harness can be awkward even for guys. It took a while going up the first few pitches because there were 4 of us, we hadn't been big wall climbing in a while and precisely zero of us were great at crack climbing. While climbing everyone drank lots of water, even more than we usually would because we were getting absolutely baked by the heat that'd dried up the route for us. Luckily the rock wasn't unbearably hot since the sun wasn't directly hitting where we were climbing or our fingers would've been real toasty. The bugs had also seemed to have gone into hiding temporarily.

The conditions were alright.

We took a break at at the start of pitch 5 right below the crux pitches. There was a nice tiny ledge about two hands width to belay from, eat a snack and - for us guys - to pee off of. It's quite an experience in itself to pee off a ledge hundreds of feet up. Unfortunately it wasn't a wide enough ledge to comfortably squat and pee off so I'm guessing that's why my friend's girlfriend didn't loosen her leg straps, drop her pants and go then. Either that or she was worried hikers would see her, although she didn't seem the sort to care about that. For anyone curious: she was wearing tight fitting light blue athletic pants, a tan t-shirt and her helmet/harness/gear. She has a very athletic build and I think she had her hair in a ponytail while climbing.

Since it was pretty featureless slab the whole way up there weren't any really convenient ledges for women to pee and the climb took us just over 7 hours, extensive lunch break included. An OK speed for a party of 4. While we were working through the crux pitches I wondered when she was going to pee because I'd been pretty bursting when I'd gone.

I found out another two hours or so later when we had the cruxes behind us and only two easy pitches left. All four of us were tied into an anchor we'd built, we'd pulled our haul bag up (really just a large backpack) and were trying to get a hanging belay in some semblance of order for the next pitch. My friend moved off to the side (so our bag and the route wouldn't get peed on) and took another pee at this anchor, then his girlfriend said she had to go too.

Up until that point she hadn't shown any signs of desperation other than a bit of "Elvis leg" which may or may not have just been calf fatigue. She traversed out to the same spot he'd peed from and we all looked away to give her a bit of privacy even though she was only a few meters away from us. A few seconds later I thought I heard a faint hissing coming from her direction, then she said she was all done way sooner than I thought she'd be.

I looked over to see her traversing back to us with the front/seat of her pants completely soaked to around her knees, it was really noticeable because of the color of her pants. She said that she'd been hoping to go after we sent the route but she couldn't hold out any longer. She'd been dying for a pee but because she'd traversed to the side with a bit of slack rather than downclimbed she found herself in a bit of a situation and her body wasn't going to give her enough time to correct it. There weren't any good holds which would've allowed her to stick her butt out from the wall or loosen her harness so she'd just peed with her pants on instead. Basically she'd done the splits (she's quite a flexible climber) to try and keep her shoes dry but with no way of getting her pants down she'd had to pee through them. Afterwards she laughed it off, said she really didn't think the logistics through but was happy she'd successfully kept her shoes dry.

We continued climbing and made faster progress, opting for dinner after we made it back since we hadn't taken headlamps with us and we were done way later than we thought we'd be. Her pants dried before we were even done the final pitch but none of us got to change into fresh clothes until we'd made it back to the van.
